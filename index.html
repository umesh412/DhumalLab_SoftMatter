Soft Matter & Complex Fluids

Understanding Soft Matter Across Scales

We investigate how molecular interactions, microstructure, and dynamics govern the macroscopic behavior of soft matter systems. Our research combines theory, coarse-grained modeling, multiscale simulation, and microstructural analysis to develop predictive frameworks for complex fluids and functional materials.
