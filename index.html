Soft Matter & Complex Fluids

Understanding Soft Matter Across Scales

We investigate how molecular interactions, microstructure, and dynamics govern the macroscopic behavior of soft matter systems. Our approach combines theoretical modeling, coarse-grained simulations, multiscale computation, and microstructural analysis to develop predictive frameworks for complex fluids and functional materials.

Soft Matter Research Themes

Research Directions

Current & Future Research

The Dhumal Lab aims to connect molecular-scale interactions with microstructure, phase behavior, transport, and macroscopic properties across diverse soft matter systems.

Pharmaceutical Soft Matter

Emulsions & Drug Precipitation

We aim to develop multiscale models of multiple emulsions as controlled environments for drug nucleation, crystallization, and precipitation. The research will connect molecular interactions, confinement, interfacial phenomena, surfactant dynamics, and transport processes to guide the rational design of pharmaceutical formulations.

Colloids

Polydisperse Colloids & Concentrated Suspensions

We combine PRISM theory with molecular and Brownian dynamics simulations to investigate how particle size distributions and interactions influence aggregation, phase transitions, microstructure, and material response in concentrated suspensions.

Functional Materials

Polymer Nanocomposites & Anisotropic Particles

We investigate how particle geometry, concentration, and polymer-particle interactions control dispersion, self-assembly, phase behavior, and structure-property relationships in polymer nanocomposites.

Biological Soft Matter

Biological & Therapeutic Soft Matter

We are developing coarse-grained and multiscale approaches for understanding interactions, organization, and transport in biological macromolecular systems. Future directions include protein-based soft matter systems such as β-lactoglobulin.

Research Approach

From Interactions to Microstructure

Our work combines theory and simulation across multiple length and time scales to connect molecular interactions with emergent structure and macroscopic behavior.

Research Foundations

Previous & Ongoing Research

Previous work provides the scientific foundation for the lab's current research program, including colloidal phase behavior, polymer nanocomposites, coarse-grained modeling, and large-scale molecular and Brownian dynamics simulations.

Colloidal Phase Separation

Theory and molecular simulations were used to investigate depletion-driven phase separation and microstructural organization in mixtures of hard colloids and soft macromolecules.

Polymer Nanocomposite Phase Behavior

PRISM theory and molecular dynamics simulations were used to study depletion, bridging, anisotropy, and polymer-mediated assembly in polymer-particle systems.

Colloidal Phase Transitions

Large-scale Brownian dynamics simulations explored liquid, crystalline, and coexisting states in concentrated colloidal systems.

Therapeutic Antibody Modeling

Coarse-grained colloidal models were used to investigate how domain-specific interactions, orientation, and steric effects influence aggregation in concentrated monoclonal antibody suspensions.
