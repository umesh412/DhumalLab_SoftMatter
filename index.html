Soft Matter & Complex Fluids

Understanding Soft Matter Across Scales

We investigate how molecular interactions, microstructure, and dynamics govern the macroscopic behavior of soft matter systems. Our approach combines theoretical modeling, coarse-grained simulations, multiscale computation, and microstructural analysis to develop predictive frameworks for complex fluids and functional materials.

Research Directions

Current & Future Research

Our research focuses on understanding and controlling structure, phase behavior, transport, and self-assembly in soft matter systems through theory and simulation.

Emulsions & Drug Precipitation

Developing multiscale models of multiple emulsions as confined environments for drug nucleation, crystallization, and precipitation. The goal is to connect molecular interactions, interfacial phenomena, surfactant behavior, and transport processes to the rational design of pharmaceutical formulations.

Polydisperse Colloids & Concentrated Suspensions

Combining PRISM theory with molecular and Brownian dynamics simulations to understand how particle-size distributions and interactions govern aggregation, phase transitions, microstructure, and material response in concentrated suspensions.

Polymer Nanocomposites & Anisotropic Particles

Investigating how particle geometry, concentration, and polymer-particle interactions control dispersion, phase behavior, self-assembly, and structure-property relationships in polymer nanocomposites.

Biological & Therapeutic Soft Matter

Developing coarse-grained and multiscale approaches for biological macromolecules and therapeutic soft matter. Current directions include studying protein interactions and microstructural organization in systems such as β-lactoglobulin.
