Understanding Soft Matter Across Scales

We investigate the structure, dynamics, and properties of soft matter and complex fluid systems through a synergistic combination of theoretical modeling, coarse-grained and multiscale simulations, and microstructural analysis.

Research Areas

Soft Matter • Colloidal Dispersions • Polymer Nanocomposites • Biological Fluids • Molecular and Coarse-Grained Simulations
