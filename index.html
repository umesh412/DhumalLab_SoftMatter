Soft Matter & Complex Fluids

Understanding Soft Matter Across Scales

We investigate how molecular and colloidal interactions give rise to microstructure, dynamics, phase behavior, transport, and macroscopic properties in complex soft materials.

By combining theory, molecular simulation, coarse-grained modeling, and multiscale analysis, we seek predictive connections between microscopic physics and the design of functional materials and formulations.

About the Lab

From Interactions to Structure and Properties

Soft matter systems occupy the space between molecular-scale interactions and macroscopic material behavior. Their properties emerge collectively from particle shape, chemistry, concentration, confinement, interfaces, and thermal motion.

The Dhumal Lab studies these connections across colloidal dispersions, polymer nanocomposites, emulsions, pharmaceutical systems, and biological soft matter. Our goal is not only to describe observed structures, but to understand the physical mechanisms that produce them and ultimately predict how they can be controlled.
