About the Lab

Bridging Interactions, Structure & Properties

The Dhumal Lab investigates the fundamental physical principles governing soft matter and complex fluids. We are particularly interested in understanding how interactions at molecular and colloidal scales give rise to collective organization, phase behavior, transport, dynamics, and macroscopic material properties.

Our long-term goal is to develop predictive frameworks that connect microscopic physics with the rational design of complex materials and formulations. These ideas are relevant to systems ranging from colloidal dispersions and polymer nanocomposites to pharmaceutical formulations, emulsions, and biological soft matter.

Research Philosophy

From Molecular Interactions to Macroscopic Behavior

We combine complementary theoretical and computational approaches across multiple length and time scales to uncover relationships between interactions, microstructure, dynamics, and material behavior.
