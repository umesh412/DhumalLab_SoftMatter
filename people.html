Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding the structure, phase behavior, dynamics, and properties of soft matter and complex fluid systems. I use theoretical and computational approaches including PRISM theory, molecular dynamics, Brownian dynamics, coarse-grained modeling, multiscale simulation, and microstructural analysis.

Current research interests include colloidal dispersions, concentrated suspensions, polymer nanocomposites, emulsions, pharmaceutical soft matter, and biological macromolecular systems.
