About

Himani Bavskar is a Ph.D. student in the Dhumal Lab with an M.Sc. in Chemistry. She is a working professional with five years of industrial experience and is currently associated with LANXESS India Pvt. Ltd.

Her industrial background complements her doctoral research in polymer nanocomposite materials.

Research Area

Reinforcement of Polymer Nanocomposites Using Anisotropic Particles

Her doctoral research focuses on the reinforcement of polymer nanocomposites using anisotropic particles, with particular emphasis on understanding how particle geometry and incorporation into the polymer matrix influence the structural properties of the resulting composite materials.
