About

Kruti Rana is a Ph.D. student in the Dhumal Lab with an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry. She brings eight years of industrial experience to her doctoral research and is currently associated with LANXESS India Pvt. Ltd.

Her industrial background complements her doctoral work in polymeric materials and their applications.

Research Area

Her doctoral research focuses on the synthesis of polymeric resins and the investigation of their properties and potential applications.
