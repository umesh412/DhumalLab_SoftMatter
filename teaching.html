Teaching Philosophy

Clarity, Structure & Engagement

My teaching philosophy is centered on presenting foundational concepts in the simplest possible form before progressing toward more complex engineering applications. I believe students develop stronger problem-solving ability when mathematical formulations are connected to physical interpretation and engineering intuition.

I organize courses using a structured week-wise plan and reinforce classroom learning through practical problem sets aligned with the topics covered during each week. Where appropriate, I supplement lectures with demonstrations, computational examples, visual material, and selected external learning resources.

I encourage students to ask questions freely, irrespective of how basic a question may appear. Active discussion helps identify conceptual gaps, increases student participation, and builds confidence with technical material.

I also aim to remain accessible outside the classroom through office hours and email communication. In assessments, I strive to be fair, transparent, consistent, and appreciative of students' effort and progress.
